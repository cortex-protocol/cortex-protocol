How to Install in 60 Seconds

No store account needed. Run fully decentralized directly in your browser.

1

Download & Extract

Download the official cortex-wallet-extension.zip and unzip the folder to your computer (e.g. into your Documents or Desktop).

2

Open Extensions & Enable Dev Mode

Open Chrome or Brave, navigate to chrome://extensions, and toggle on the "Developer mode" switch in the top right corner.

3

Click "Load Unpacked"

Click the "Load unpacked" button in the top left, select the unzipped Cortex Wallet folder, and pin the wallet icon to your toolbar!
